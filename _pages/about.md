Welcome! My name is Seoyun Hong, and I also go by Stella. 

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Economics at Georgia State University. I received my PhD in Economics from Boston University in 2026. 

<p style="text-align: justify; text-justify: inter-word;">
My research interests are broadly in econometrics, including machine learning, high-dimensional data, and quantile/distributional methods. I am also interested in applied econometrics projects that develop estimators tailored to specific empirical questions. In this work, I have examined the gender wage gap across work hours and the effects of collusion on prices in procurement auctions.
</p>
